# Brag Plan: TeleBots

## What is this app?
TeleBots is a Ukrainian studio site for turnkey websites, Telegram chatbots, and e-commerce — from free consult to launch, with CRM, payments, and 200+ shipped projects.

## The angle
Not another "digital agency." It's the shop that builds the bot that takes the order while you sleep — then the site that converts the rest. Specific claim: sites + Telegram bots under one roof, fast start in 24 hours, ready in 2 weeks.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Huge Montserrat-style uppercase on black: **«БОТ БЕРЕ ЗАЯВКУ»** — then a pink brand slash and **«НАВІТЬ О 3 НОЧІ»**. Instant business pain + product answer.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Hero reveal: real TeleBots headline — «Розробка сайтів і Telegram-ботів під ключ» over the dusk city hero vibe (dark gradient + brand pink).
- Stats beat: **200+** проєктів / **4+** роки / старт **за 24 години**.
- Services snap: Чат-боти · Сайти · E-commerce — three soft pink cards, one by one.

## Outro / punchline
Logo **telebots.** + line: «Заявка → бот → оплата.» CTA: telebots.site

## User flow worth showing
Landing consult flow: open site → leave a lead («Обговоримо проєкт») → get a clear scenario, timeline, and price — product is the studio website itself (marketing + lead capture), not an in-app product.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium agency film with one sharp Ukrainian hook
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, confident type, restrained motion, brand pink as the only loud color
